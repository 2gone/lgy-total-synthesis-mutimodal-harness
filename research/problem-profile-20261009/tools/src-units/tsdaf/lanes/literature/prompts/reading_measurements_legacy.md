Measurements. Decide whether a reported result is the same measurement as an existing result: the same product and experimental run, not merely equal numbers. When it is, select its supplied name in that yield's words.same_measurement_as while saving the full expression; use the finding's explanation for the reason. Do not make a separate single-measurement pairing or repeat that reason. Product candidates are only navigation hints, never identity decisions. Another experiment, another scale or uncertain identity stays unpaired with a concrete explanation. Legacy same_measurements remains readable and can retain an explicitly identified overall-yield combination with all its span members; such spans are not published as a single existing measurement. Do not pair only the last product or flatten a span to make it publish.
