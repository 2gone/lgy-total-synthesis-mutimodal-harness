# Roadmap

## Dependencies

Each slice consumes stable contracts, immutable artifacts, and receipts established by the slices before it. Later capabilities must not bypass the standard-package boundary or reconstruct project premises from chat history.

## Slice 1

Build the weak-article Router foundation: package boundary, canonicalization, text-first routing,
isolated visual triage, immutable handoff, local Runner, offline tests, and five-paper gold
validation. The offline foundation and Task 12 five-paper explicit-live validation are complete.
The current boundary is tracked by [WI-0001](work/WI-0001-weak-article-router-v1.md).

## Image processing v1 foundation

The release-gated local image worker foundation is implemented ahead of scaled orchestration:
verified source materialization, external MolYOLO/YOLOv10, normalized coordinates, deterministic
crops, and one-crop Gemini OCSR. Real-weight/GPU and remote OCSR canaries, Semantic Binder,
molecule binding, and final Image Package construction remain future gates.

## Slice 2

Add review workflows, adjudication, durable review receipts, and Gold-200 calibration after Slice 1 contracts and handoff semantics are sealed.

## Slice 3

Build strong route-text packaging and route-level narrative alignment on top of accepted handoffs and canonical block identities.

## Slice 4

Add the supporting-information lane, experimental procedures, entity linking, and main-paper/SI provenance connections after route-text contracts stabilize.

## Slice 5

Operationalize the current image worker with orchestration adapters, controlled canaries, binding,
review, and scaled rollout only after blind-gold quality and receipt gates pass.

## Follow-on direction

Scale from five papers to Gold-200, Gold-100 blind, a 1k canary, and then 10k literature processing. Halt rollout when visual escalation or provenance failures exceed the accepted thresholds; repair canonicalization, caption linking, taxonomy, or prompts before resuming.
